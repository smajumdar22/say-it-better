How this works

Each day you learn a few new words and review the ones that are due. Words you get right come back less often (1, 2, 4, 7, 15, 30, then 60 days). Words you miss come back tomorrow. Questions get harder as you improve: first multiple choice, then fill-in-the-blank, then typing the word from memory. Use the AI coach to check your own sentences, because using a word yourself is what makes it stick.
